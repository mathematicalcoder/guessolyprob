Conventions

  • For PMO Qualifying/Areas, the problem number format is Part.No, for example, if the problem is Part I Problem 3, then one must enter I.3 in the problem number field.
  • For PMO contests, the year is the corresponding year of the national stage.
  • For MATHirang MATHibay, enter E[No.] for eliminations, T[Tier]-[No.] for orals, and W[Wave]-[No.] for finals.
